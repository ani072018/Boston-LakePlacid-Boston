ADIRONDACK NORTHWAYEN ROUTE

A drive wrapped in autumn's glow

The road wound through dense forests dressed in every shade of fall — fiery reds, burnt oranges, and glowing yellows that seemed to stretch endlessly. Sunlight filtered through the canopy, scattering gold across the highway, while crisp mountain air filled the car with the scent of pine and earth.

"It was one of those drives where time didn't matter — just the feeling of freedom, and the anticipation of reaching Lake Placid nestled in the heart of the mountains."

The deeper we drove into the Adirondacks, the quieter it became. The hustle of the city felt miles away, replaced by the rhythm of rustling leaves and the hum of tires on open road.

VETERAN'S MEMORIAL HWYASCENDING

Rising above autumn's canvas

We began our ascent up the mountain via the veteran highway, one of the most scenic drives we've ever experienced. The road winds gracefully through dense forests and gentle hills, each turn revealing a new perspective — trees blazing red, orange, gold, sunlight streaming through branches like a natural kaleidoscope.

The higher we climbed, the more expansive the views became — valleys, distant peaks, and sparkling lakes below, as if we were moving through a living painting.

Elevation
The Veteran's Memorial Highway climbs roughly 3,500 feet in under five miles — one of the steepest paved ascents in the Northeast.

SUMMIT — WHITEFACE MOUNTAIN

4,867 feet, two lakes below

Reaching the top was a reward in itself. From the summit, the view was absolutely mesmerizing — two lakes stretched out beneath us, their waters reflecting the brilliant fall colors that surrounded them. Standing there, taking it all in, it was clear the journey along the highway and up the mountain was just as unforgettable as the view itself.

Autumn moments on the way back

We took time to stroll through the charming streets, exploring cozy shops and soaking in the autumn atmosphere. After a hearty dinner, we began the long drive back to Boston, retracing the roads that had offered such vibrant color, peaceful mountain views, and one quiet, unexpected encounter with wildlife — a perfect blend of adventure, nature, and the simple moments that make a road trip unforgettable.
